# How to put a car on the website

This is how to add a car to the **Cars for sale** page on www.jgauto.co.uk, change it, or mark it as sold. You do it all in a web page called **Pages CMS**. You can use a computer, tablet or phone.

You can't break the website by doing this. If something looks wrong, just change it back, or ring Alex.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and type in your GitHub username and password.
   (The first time, it may ask you to allow Pages CMS. Click **Authorize**.)
3. Click **JG-Auto-Site**.
4. Click **Cars for sale** on the left-hand side.

You'll see a list of every car that's on the website.

> Tip: save app.pagescms.org as a bookmark so it's easy to find next time.

---

## Adding a new car

1. Click **Add an entry** (at the bottom of the list).
2. Fill in the boxes:

| Box | What to put in |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Pick **For sale** |
| **Make** | e.g. Ford |
| **Model** | e.g. Focus 1.6 Zetec |
| **Year** | e.g. 2015 |
| **Price (£)** | Numbers only, e.g. **4995** (no £ sign, no commas). Leave it empty to show **POA**. |
| **Mileage** | Numbers only, e.g. **84000** |
| **Fuel** | Pick from the list |
| **Gearbox** | Manual or Automatic |
| **MOT expiry** | Pick the date the MOT runs out |
| **Description** | A few lines about the car: condition, history, owners, anything new fitted |
| **Photos** | See "Adding photos" below |

3. Click **Save** (top right). **Nothing goes on the website until you click Save.**

The car will appear on the website a minute or two later. If you don't see it, wait a couple of minutes and refresh the page.

---

## Adding photos

1. In the **Photos** box, click the **+** (or **Add**) button.
2. Click **Upload** and choose photos from your computer or phone. You can pick several at once.
3. Click the photos you want for this car so they're ticked, then click **Select** / **Insert**.
4. **The first photo is the main one** people see. You can drag the photos into a different order.
5. Remember to click **Save**.

Good photos to take: front three-quarter view, back, both sides, the dashboard showing the mileage, the seats, and the boot. Up to 12 photos per car.

Photos straight from a phone are fine. Take them in daylight with the car clean if you can.

---

## Changing the price or details

1. Sign in and click **Cars for sale**.
2. Click on the car in the list.
3. Change what you need.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## When a car is sold

1. Click on the car.
2. Change **Status** to **Sold**.
3. Click **Save**.

The car stays on the website with a **SOLD** label (that's good for showing customers that cars sell). The price is hidden.

## Taking a car off the website completely

- Change **Status** to **Hidden** and click **Save**. The car disappears from the website but you can bring it back later by changing it to **For sale**.
- Or, to delete it for good, click the **bin** icon next to that car in the list and then **Save**.

---

## Something not right?

- **I clicked Save but can't see the change.** Wait two minutes, then refresh the website page. On a phone, pull down on the page to refresh.
- **It says I don't have access.** Ring Alex.
- **I made a mistake.** Just change it back and click **Save** again.
