# How to put a car on the website

The cars on **www.jgauto.co.uk/cars.html** are added through a free website called
**Pages CMS**. It works on a computer or in your phone's web browser.

## Signing in

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org**
2. Sign in with your email address (the one you were invited with).
3. Open **JG-Auto-Site**, then **Cars for sale** on the left.

Tip: on your phone, add the page to your home screen so it's one tap next time.

## Adding a car

1. Tap **Add an entry**, usually at the bottom of the list.
2. Fill in the boxes:
   - **Status:** choose **For sale**
   - **Make**, **Model**, **Year**
   - **Price:** numbers only, like `4995`. Leave it empty and the website shows **POA**.
   - **Mileage:** numbers only, like `64000`
   - **Fuel**, **Gearbox**, **Engine**, **Colour**, **MOT expiry**: fill in what you know. Anything left blank just doesn't show.
   - **Description:** a few lines about the car, like history, condition and extras.
3. **Photos:** tap to add, then pick them from your phone. The **first photo is the
   main one**, so make it a good one of the whole car.
4. Press **Save**.

The car appears on the website after a minute or two. Refresh the page if you don't see it.

## When a car sells

Open the car, change **Status** to **Sold**, and press **Save**.
It stays on the website with a red **SOLD** label, which shows people you sell cars.

To take it off the website completely, change Status to **Hidden** instead, or
delete it from the list.

## Not ready yet?

Set **Status** to **Hidden** while you're still getting photos or details together.
It won't show on the website until you change it to **For sale**.

## Photo tips

- Take them in daylight, with the phone turned sideways (landscape).
- Keep the whole car in the picture with a bit of space around it.
- Good ones to take: front corner, back corner, side, dashboard (showing mileage),
  front seats, back seats, boot.
- No need to make photos smaller first. The website shrinks them automatically.

## Something gone wrong?

Nothing you do here can break the rest of the website. If a car looks wrong, open
it, fix it and save again. If you're stuck, give Alex a shout.
